⚡ Features

Everything your finance team needs

From CSV to inbox in seconds. FinCredit AI handles the full credit follow-up lifecycle so your team can focus on strategy, not admin.

📊
Smart Invoice Processing
Reads your invoice CSV, validates every record, detects overdue status, and calculates exact days past due. Handles messy data gracefully.
🎯
4-Stage Escalation Engine
Automatically assigns Friendly → Formal → Urgent → Legal escalation stages based on days overdue and previous reminder count.
🤖
AI-Personalised Emails
GPT-4o via LangChain writes personalised, legally-appropriate emails for each client and stage. Never a generic template again.
📧
SMTP Email Sending
Sends real emails via Gmail or any SMTP provider. Dry-run mode lets you preview all emails before a single one goes out.
💾
Complete Audit Trail
Every action logged to SQLite + JSON. Full audit history with email subject, body, send status, and model used. Never lose a record.
🔒
Secure by Design
.env for all secrets, zero hardcoded credentials, input validation on every field, and session-based dashboard authentication.
🔄 Process

From CSV to inbox in 4 steps

The agent runs the entire pipeline automatically — or you control every step via the dashboard.

1
Load Invoices
Point the agent at your invoices CSV. It validates every field and reports any data issues.
2
Detect & Classify
Overdue invoices are identified and assigned an escalation stage based on days past due.
3
AI Generates Email
LangChain + GPT-4o writes a personalised, stage-appropriate email for each client contact.
4
Send & Log
Emails are sent via SMTP and every event is logged to SQLite + JSON for full auditability.
⚠️ Escalation

The right tone at the right time

AI calibrates every email's language, urgency, and legal framing based on how overdue the invoice is.

Stage 1
1–14 days
Friendly Reminder
Warm, helpful tone. Assumes an honest oversight. No urgency language.
Stage 2
15–29 days
Formal Notice
Professional and firm. References prior reminders. Requests immediate action.
Stage 3
30–59 days
Urgent Escalation
Serious tone. Mentions potential credit hold or service suspension. 5-day deadline.
Stage 4
60+ days
Final Legal Warning
Zero ambiguity. Clear 48-hour deadline. States referral to legal/collections department.
